Happy Birthday,
October Girl.

✿

Ohh, it’s October again. Almost touching winter. October and November always make us eagerly wait for the winter to come.

These months remind me of foggy winter mornings— morning tea, newspapers, and morning walks.

I think you’re blessed to have been born in a month like this.

As far as I know and can guess, you’re a very raw and genuine person. There’s no double intention in the way you speak or behave. You say things directly, and honestly, that’s something I find really cute. Always be like this.

I’m not going to simply say, “Hey, Happy Birthday!” or write the usual birthday wishes and bla bla bla. 😄 Instead, I just want to say that I genuinely appreciate you, and on your birthday, I’ll keep you in my prayers.

You love flowers so much, so I hope one day you’ll have a big garden full of flowers, InshaAllah. 🌸

And, of course, there will be rabbits playing around in that garden too—including the big rabbit, Momo. 🐇

May this new year of your life bring you peaceful mornings, beautiful moments, sincere people, and all the little things that make you genuinely happy. May Allah keep you blessed, protect your smile, and give you many reasons to smile in the days ahead.

Happy Birthday. 🤍

Welcome, October girl. 🌸
